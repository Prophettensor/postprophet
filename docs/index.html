The loop

Connect to your agent stack. Ideas surface. Content is generated, posted, and learned from performance.

1HarvestConnectors pull grounded "seeds" from your stack — git commits, research outputs, customer signals, ecosystem events.no LLM
2IdeasSeeds are framed against your vision and market and ranked into content ideas. What shipped, why it matters, to whom.no LLM
3PlanWinning strategies are paired with the best-fit ideas by a bandit that learns what works for your audience.no LLM
4DraftAn agent turns each idea and strategy into a post, grounded only in your real facts. Coach or auto.LLM
5PublishYou review and post (coach), or the agent posts on schedule (auto).you choose
6LearnReal engagement reweights the strategies. The playbook adapts to what actually works — not vibes.no LLM

Why now

X open-sourced its For You feed ranking algorithm. That gave content agents a real objective.

1

A real reward function

X's shipped weights tell us what it values: reply 5.0 (→20 mutual), quote 5.0, share 2.0, follow 4.0, favorite 0.5. PostProphet engineers drafts toward those actions.

2

Learns from real results

The reward comes from actual post metrics, not a model scoring its own work. Non-circular: no vibes, no self-ratings. What performs is what wins.

3

Understands your vision

Not a generic bot. PostProphet connects to your stack, reads your positioning and market, and surfaces ideas about what you're actually building.

Strategies

Each draft is engineered toward a high-value action, from X's weights. The learner decides which to favor.

reply_starter quote_worthy share_worthy dwell_holder follow_bait

Each × mutual (your engaged audience) or out_of_network (new readers — the way you grow).
