How it works

Not a dashboard. Not analytics. A prediction harness that helps you post better.

1

Write your tweet

Draft your tweet and set a target — how many impressions you want, and in what timeframe.

2

Get a prediction

PostProphet scores your tweet on 8 dimensions — hook, specificity, emotional trigger, reply inducement, bookmark worthiness, structure, clarity, and link risk. It sees your account history, your ecosystem, and analyzes attached media.

3

Iterate until it ships

Low probability? PostProphet gives directive feedback — not "add a personal touch" but "Open with 'We just shipped...' — match your best tweet's structure." Use the craft loop to iterate automatically.

The eval

Real tweets. Real impressions. Real scores.

BRIER SCORE

Standard forecasting metric

How well does the probability match reality? 0 = perfect, 1 = worst. Change the harness, rerun, compare scores.

2X MEDIAN

Aspirational target grounded in reality

Each tweet predicts against 2x the author's median impressions — will this tweet perform twice as well as their typical tweet? Grounded in real history, not a fixed number.

NO LEAKAGE

Prediction tweet's engagement is hidden

The model never sees the tweet's own likes, replies, or bookmarks. Ecosystem tweets posted after the prediction are filtered out. It predicts as if the tweet hasn't been posted yet.

24H RESOLVE

Resolves at the tweet's actual 24h mark

95% of tweets stop getting impressions after 24 hours. Predictions resolve at posted_at + 24h, not prediction time + 24h.
