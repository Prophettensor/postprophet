The problem

Every other tool tells you what happened after you posted. PostProphet tells you before.

1

You can't unpost

Once it's live, it's live. A bad tweet doesn't just flop. It's seen by followers, indexed, and remembered. There's no delete key that undoes the impression.

2

Analytics is hindsight

Engagement dashboards show you what already happened. Great for post-mortems, useless for the tweet you're about to write right now.

3

Gut feel isn't scaleable

Humans get it right sometimes. Agents posting on your behalf need more than a hunch. They need a test they can run before shipping.

How it works

Not a dashboard. Not analytics. A prediction you can act on.

1

Draft your tweet

Write your tweet and tell PostProphet your handle. It pulls your real performance history to ground the prediction in your actual track record.

2

Get a prediction

You get a prediction on how the tweet will perform and specific feedback. Not "be more engaging" but "name what you shipped, drop the comparison, match the structure of your best tweet."

3

Revise until ready

Revise based on the feedback, re-predict, and watch it climb. The craft loop can run this automatically. Write, edit, repeat until the prediction is high enough to ship.

Grounded in reality

Not a vibe check. Real tweets, real impressions, real scoring.

REAL DATA

Trained on your actual history

PostProphet pulls your real tweet performance. What worked, what didn't, your best and worst. The prediction is grounded in your actual track record, not a generic formula.

FEEDBACK

Actionable, not generic

Not "be more engaging." Specific, directive feedback. Name what you shipped, drop the comparison, match the structure of your best-performing tweet. Change this, not that.

ITERATIVE

Loop until it's ready

Draft, predict, revise, repeat. The craft loop runs automatically. Your agent writes, PostProphet edits, the tweet improves with each round. Ship when the prediction is high enough.
