How it works: The Market Research Agent monitors the ecosystem daily, producing a brief of what's hot. The Dev Intelligence (main agent) writes a brief of what happened in development and why it matters. The Content Agent takes both briefs and drafts tweets. PostProphet evaluates each draft with probability + scores + feedback. The Content Agent revises based on feedback. This loops until confidence hits 40%, plateaus, or hits 5 iterations max. The Dev Intelligence reviews the winner. The human gives final approval. Then it posts.
